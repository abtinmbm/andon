Generated by `andon.jidoka report` from `results/h6_jidoka_id_00.txt`, `results/h6_jidoka_id_01.txt`, `results/h6_jidoka_id_02.txt`. Times are from the board's clock: the switch is the speed command's arrival, red the trip, and fan off the gate pin reading back low (the fan's power cut; spin-down is not timed). Red is stamped at the start of the loop pass that scored the deciding window, so red to fan off is mostly that window's log-mel and GMM score; the fan is cut before anything is printed. Each fan calibrated on its own first 584 windows after a 5 s hold-off, ran 60 s watched, then was switched from 100 % to 85 % (underspeed) by a serial command.
